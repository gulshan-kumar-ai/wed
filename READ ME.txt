PAMOORA WEDDING INVITATION
==========================

THE CARD ONLINE
  https://gulshan-kumar-ai.github.io/wed/
  To view it offline, double-click "Wedding Invitation.html" (Chrome or
  Edge recommended), tap "Open the Invitation" and scroll down. An internet
  connection is needed for the fonts and the Google Maps.

LANGUAGES
  Guests choose English, Urdu or Sindhi on the opening doors, or later with
  the globe button in the top corner. Please ask someone who reads Urdu and
  Sindhi well to check the wording before the card goes out. The wording is
  in source\src\config\wedding.i18n.js and source\src\strings.js.

PERSONAL LINKS
  Add the guest's name (and party size) to the link to greet them by name:
    https://gulshan-kumar-ai.github.io/wed/?to=Sharma+Family&n=4
  "Dear Sharma Family" appears on the doors and their RSVP is filled in.

GOOGLE SHEET: RSVPs AND VISITS
  Until the Sheet is connected, each reply opens in WhatsApp addressed to
  Prem U. Pamoora (+92 332 8209460), ready for the guest to send.
  Once it is connected, every reply AND every opening of the invitation
  is recorded in one Google Sheet:
    Summary      live totals: times opened, different phones, replies,
                 accepted, declined, guests coming, head count per
                 ceremony, pick-ups and rooms needed
    RSVPs        one row per guest reply
    Visits       one row each time someone opens the invitation
    Invitations  each personal link: how often it was opened, and whether
                 that guest has replied
  To connect it (about 5 minutes, once):
    1. Open source\rsvp\google-apps-script.gs and follow the steps at the
       top: create the Sheet, paste the script, run "setup", deploy it.
    2. Put the web-app link it gives you (it ends in /exec) into
       source\src\config\wedding.js at rsvp.endpoint, then publish (below).
  The WhatsApp number for replies is rsvp.whatsapp in the same file
  (international format without +, e.g. '923328209460').

PUBLISHING CHANGES
  In the source folder run:   npm run release
  then commit and push. GitHub Pages updates the site within a minute.
  (Changing files in source\ alone does not change the live card.)

FILES
  index.html                        The live card (what GitHub Pages shows).
  Wedding Invitation.html           The same card, to open with a double-click.
  favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png Small icons used by the card (keep them next to it).
  og-image.jpg                      The picture WhatsApp shows when the link is shared.
  audio\                            The background music: a sitar & tabla raga under a
                                    free licence (credit in the footer, details in
                                    audio\README.txt). Film songs are copyrighted.
  source\                           The editable project: run "npm install" and
                                    "npm run dev" inside it to make changes.
