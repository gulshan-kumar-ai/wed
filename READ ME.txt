PAMOORA WEDDING INVITATION
==========================

TO VIEW THE CARD
  Double-click "Wedding Invitation.html". It opens in your web browser
  (Chrome or Edge recommended). Tap "Open the Invitation" and scroll down.
  An internet connection is needed for the fonts and the Google Maps.

LANGUAGES
  Guests choose English, Urdu or Sindhi on the opening doors (or in the
  footer). Please ask someone who reads Urdu and Sindhi well to check the
  wording before the card goes out. The wording is in
  source\src\config\wedding.i18n.js and source\src\strings.js.

PERSONAL LINKS
  Add the guest's name (and party size) to the link to greet them by name:
    https://gulshan-kumar-ai.github.io/wed/?to=Sharma+Family&n=4
  "Dear Sharma Family" appears on the doors and their RSVP is filled in.

HOW RSVPs REACH YOU
  Right now each reply opens in WhatsApp, ready for the guest to send to
  whoever invited them. To collect every reply in one Google Sheet instead:
    1. Open source\rsvp\google-apps-script.gs and follow the steps at the
       top (about 5 minutes).
    2. Paste the web-app link it gives you into source\src\config\wedding.js
       at rsvp.endpoint, then rebuild the card.
  To have WhatsApp replies addressed to one family number, put it at
  rsvp.whatsapp in the same file (for example '923001234567').

FILES
  Wedding Invitation.html           The complete card in a single file.
  favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png Small icons used by the card (keep them next to it).
  Invitation Preview Image.jpg      The picture WhatsApp shows when the link is shared.
  audio\                            Put a shehnai/sitar track here named "ambient.mp3"
                                    to replace the built-in soft drone music.
  source\                           The editable project: run "npm install" and
                                    "npm run dev" inside it to make changes.

SHARING ON WHATSAPP
  To send guests a link, the card must be put online (for example on GitHub
  Pages, Netlify or Vercel). Sending the .html file itself on WhatsApp will
  not open nicely on phones.

STILL TO FILL IN (in source\src\config\wedding.js)
  - Spellings "Grouve" and "Prena"
  - Ceremony days/times and the phere muhurat. Guests see them marked
    "provisional" until you set timingsConfirmed: true.
  - Coordinator names and phone numbers (hidden from guests until added)
  - Photos: portraits and gallery (the gallery stays hidden until added)
  - The Google Sheet link and/or family WhatsApp number for RSVPs (see above)
