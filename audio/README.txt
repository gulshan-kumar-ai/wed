ambient.mp3 is the background music: "Raga Kaushi Kanra" by Ranjit Makkuni (sitar)
with Akram Khan (tabla), from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RanjitMakkuniRagaKaushiKanra.ogg).
Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
Edited for the invitation: mono, softer level, 2.5-second fades for a seamless loop.
The edited file is shared under the same licence; the footer shows the credit.

To use other music, replace ambient.mp3 here AND in source\public\audio, and
update music.credit in source\src\config\wedding.js. Without the file, the
music button plays a soft synthesized tanpura drone.
